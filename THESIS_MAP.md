# Where the thesis gets its numbers

Every table and figure of the thesis, and the notebook section whose stored
output holds its numbers. A section number means the section of that notebook,
so `06b` section 27 is section 27 of `notebooks/06b_study2_supplements.ipynb`.
The numbering follows the version of the thesis submitted with this repository.
